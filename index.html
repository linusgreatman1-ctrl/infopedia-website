About Infopedia

A technology partner built by engineers, for builders.

Infopedia Technology was founded by a team of engineers, data specialists and educators who kept running into the same problem: most companies are sold technology, not a plan for using it. We started as a software house and grew into a full technology partner — one that can design an AI model, wire up the database behind it, build the interface people actually use, and then train a client's own staff to carry it forward.

Today that shows up as work across artificial intelligence, software and web engineering, data infrastructure, content strategy, and hands-on training through our Tech Academy — delivered from our head office in Lagos and teams in Abuja, Port Harcourt, Benin City and Minneapolis.

Vision

To be recognised globally for technology solutions that are genuinely used, not just delivered — reshaping how businesses and individuals put technology to work.

Mission

To close the gap between technology and business outcomes with solutions tailored to each client's operations, so efficiency, growth and capability compound over time.

What we build

Six disciplines. One accountable team.

Each service stands on its own, and each one is stronger for sitting next to the others — the same team that trains your model can also build the database and the interface around it.

We build machine learning, natural language and computer vision systems that plug into decisions your business already makes — not proofs of concept that stall after the demo.

  • Predictive models for forecasting, anomaly detection and customer segmentation
  • Chatbots, virtual assistants and sentiment analysis over NLP
  • Computer vision for monitoring, quality control and recognition systems

Bespoke applications, enterprise systems and the cross-platform desktop and mobile apps that put them in front of users — built to fit how your business actually operates, not the other way round.

  • Custom applications, ERP/CRM builds and secure API development
  • Cross-platform mobile apps (React Native, Flutter) and native desktop software
  • Integration with the systems you already run, plus staff onboarding

Responsive, fast, easy-to-maintain websites and e-commerce platforms — designed to represent the brand accurately and convert visitors, not just look good in a pitch deck.

  • Custom, responsive design tuned for every device
  • E-commerce builds with secure payments and inventory management
  • CMS implementation (WordPress, Drupal and others) your team can run without us

Data is only an asset if it's structured, secure and fast to query. We design the systems underneath your product so the rest of the stack has something solid to stand on.

  • Relational and non-relational design, tuned for scale
  • Migration from legacy systems with validated, zero-loss cutovers
  • Ongoing monitoring, backup and recovery support

The best-built platform still needs words, video and search visibility that match its quality. Our content team works alongside engineering, not after it.

  • Copywriting for web, product and marketing, in your brand voice
  • Video, animation and multimedia production
  • SEO-driven content strategy with ongoing performance reporting

We built our Academy for the same reason we build client software: skills should be transferable, not locked inside one team. Courses run on real projects, not slide decks.

  • Programming, web development and data-science tracks
  • Project-based learning with mentorship from working engineers
  • Bootcamps and workshops for teams upskilling in-house

How we work

Client-centric, agile, and honest about trade-offs.

We start by understanding the problem you're actually trying to solve — which is not always the one in the first email. From there we work in short, visible iterations so you see progress and can redirect early, rather than waiting for a single "big reveal" at the end.

Discovery first

We map the goal, the constraints and the people involved before writing a line of code or a spec.

Agile delivery

Work ships in stages you can review, so scope and budget stay predictable throughout.

Built to hand over

Documentation and training are part of delivery, not an afterthought — your team can run what we build.

Support that continues

Maintenance, monitoring and updates carry on after launch, on terms agreed up front.

Leadership

The people behind the work.

Linus Edeigba

Founder & CEO

Linus is the founder and lead developer behind Infopedia Technology, bringing a multidisciplinary background in Applied Science and Human Psychology together with advanced technical training in software development, artificial intelligence and digital technologies completed across Nigeria and the United States between 2016 and 2018. He leads development of intelligent educational and enterprise software across web and mobile platforms, overseeing product design, AI integration and system architecture for Infopedia's growing portfolio of Nigerian-focused digital products spanning edtech, mobility, marketplace and fintech. He's equally invested in tech education, driving digital skill development programmes across Nigeria — building the systems and the people who will carry them forward.

Desmond Ikpefua

Co-founder & Head of Legal

Desmond is an accomplished lawyer specialising in space law. He graduated with honours from Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife in 2014, then earned a Distinction in his Master's in Space Law from Northumbria University, Newcastle. He has since advised space agencies, companies and governments on legal issues spanning space exploration, satellite communications and space tourism, and is the author of Outer Space Commercialisation: A Critical Analysis of the Legal Regime. His expertise is widely recognised, and he is regularly invited to speak at international conferences and seminars on space law. Desmond is committed to bridging space exploration and space sustainability, and brings that same rigour to shaping Infopedia's future.

Bright Enos

Head of Administration

Bright serves as Head of Administration at Infopedia Technology, combining a Business Administration degree with extensive experience across both corporate and startup environments. He manages operations and ensures the company's strategic objectives are met efficiently, drawing on a background in business consultation to identify opportunities for improvement and implement best practice across the organisation. Bright's tech-savvy approach means administrative processes run on the same innovative software solutions Infopedia builds for clients, and his eye for detail keeps the company's day-to-day operations running smoothly so the wider team can focus on delivering for clients.
